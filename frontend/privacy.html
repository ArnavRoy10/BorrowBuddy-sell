Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us including your name, email address, username, and profile information. We also collect information about your use of the platform including items listed, requests made, and messages sent.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, facilitate transactions between users, communicate with you about your account, send you updates and notifications, and ensure platform security and prevent fraud.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as necessary to provide our services, such as displaying your profile to other users, facilitating transactions, or as required by law.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers hosted on MongoDB Atlas. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored in plain text. We also store some preferences locally in your browser for a better experience.

5. Security

We implement security measures to protect your information including JWT authentication, password hashing, and input sanitization. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings. You can also request account deletion by contacting us.

7. Cookies

We use browser storage to maintain your login session and preferences. These are essential for the platform to function properly.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at arnavbhi5@gmail.com
