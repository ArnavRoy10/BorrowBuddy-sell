About BorrowBuddy

Our Mission

BorrowBuddy was built with one goal: help people save money by borrowing what they need, and help others earn by lending items they don't use. Why buy something you'll use once when your neighbour has one sitting idle?

Why BorrowBuddy Exists

Every household has items collecting dust — drills, cameras, camping gear, textbooks, appliances. At the same time, people nearby need exactly those things but don't want to spend money buying them. BorrowBuddy connects both sides: borrowers save money, lenders earn from what they already own. Everyone wins.

Our Values

Save Money

Borrow what you need at a fraction of the cost of buying. Perfect for items you only need once or twice.

Earn from What You Own

List items you rarely use and earn money every time someone borrows them. Turn idle possessions into passive income.

Trust & Safety

Secure payments via Razorpay, a built-in dispute system, and verified user accounts keep every transaction safe.

Less Waste

Sharing means fewer unnecessary purchases and less environmental waste. Good for your wallet, good for the planet.
