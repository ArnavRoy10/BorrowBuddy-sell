Frequently Asked Questions

How does BorrowBuddy work?
BorrowBuddy connects people who have items to lend with those who need to borrow them. Simply browse available items, send a borrow request, and arrange pickup with the owner. It's that simple!
Is it safe to lend my items?
Yes! We have several safety features including user verification, security deposits, ratings and reviews, and in-app messaging. You always have control over who borrows your items.
How much does it cost?
Creating an account and browsing items is completely free! Lenders set their own rental prices, and you only pay when you actually borrow an item.
What if an item gets damaged?
Security deposits protect lenders from damage or loss. If an item is damaged, the security deposit can be used to cover repairs or replacement. We recommend taking photos before and after each borrowing period.
How do I set prices for my items?
You can set your own daily rental price and security deposit. Consider the item's value, replacement cost, and typical rental rates in your area.
Can I borrow for multiple days?
Yes! You can specify the borrowing period when sending a request. The total cost is calculated based on the daily rate multiplied by the number of days.
How do I contact other users?
Once a request is approved, you can communicate with the other user through our in-app messaging system to arrange pickup, return, and discuss any details.
What types of items can I list?
You can list almost anything! Common categories include electronics, books, tools, sports equipment, camping gear, party supplies, and more. Prohibited items include weapons, illegal substances, and hazardous materials.
